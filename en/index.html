01 What we do

Services

All hairdressing services for women, from a cut and blow-dry to a complete colour change.

  1. 01

    Cuts and styling

    Women's haircuts, blow-dries, curls and straightening, for every day and for special occasions.

  2. 02

    Colour and highlights

    Full colour, highlights, balayage and lightening, from blondes and silvers to coppers and reds.

  3. 03

    Creative and fashion colour

    Pastel and neon shades, pink, violet, blue, rainbow colour. What we are known for.

  4. 04

    Occasion and bridal hair

    Updos, braids and waves for weddings, proms and celebrations.

  5. 05

    Hair care

    Care and treatments for hair after colouring and lightening, with professional Nook, Cotril and Montibello products.

02 Who we are

About us

You will be welcomed by Ivanka, the owner, and Valentina.

We do all hairdressing services, and colour is what we love most: from caring for a natural shade to the pastel and neon tones you will see in the gallery. We work with professional products from Nook, Cotril and Montibello.
